# Competition Formats Research — Real-World Structural Facts

Date: 2026-10-21
Purpose: capture the structural facts needed to model a football manager game: team counts,
qualification paths, group/league-phase formats, knockout formats, and per-country spot allocation.
All facts below reflect the formats in force as of the 2025/26–2026/27 seasons. Sources are cited
per competition; primary sources (UEFA/FIFA/confederation regulations and official pages) preferred.

Notable format changes to be aware of (summary):

- **UEFA club competitions (from 2024/25):** Champions League, Europa League and Conference League
  all replaced 32-team group stages with a **36-team single-league "league phase"** (Swiss model).
  No cross-competition transfer after the league phase (CL/EL losers no longer drop down).
- **FIFA World Cup (from 2026):** expanded to **48 teams**, 12 groups of 4, and a new **Round of 32**;
  104 matches. OFC gets a guaranteed direct slot for the first time.
- **FIFA Club World Cup (from 2025):** expanded from 7 to **32 teams**, held every 4 years.
- **UEFA Champions League:** two extra **European Performance Spots** (one each for the two
  best-performing associations of the previous season).
- **AFC Champions League Elite:** expands from **24 to 32** teams from the 2026/27 edition.
- **Africa Cup of Nations:** expands from **24 to 28** teams from the 2027 edition.
- **UEFA Nations League:** transitions from a four-league (A/B/C/D) to a **three-league (18-team)** system for 2028/29.

## 1. UEFA — Club Competitions

### 1.1 UEFA Champions League (format since 2024/25; current 2025/26)

- **Teams in league phase:** 36 (single unified league table, no groups).
- **League phase format:** each club plays **8 matches** — 4 home, 4 away — against **8 different
  opponents** (drawn into two opponents from each of 4 seeding pots, no same-country pairing,
  max 2 opponents from any one country). Total competition matches rose 125 → **189**.
- **Qualification from league phase:**
  - **1st–8th** → Round of 16 (bye).
  - **9th–24th** → two-legged **knockout phase play-offs**; 8 winners join R16.
  - **25th–36th** → eliminated (no drop to Europa League).
- **Knockout:** R16 → quarter-finals → semi-finals → final, all two-legged except single-match final.
  Minimum 8 games, maximum 17 for a finalist.
- **Qualification into the league phase (2025/26, 29 direct + 7 via qualifying):**
  - Champions League title holders (1), Europa League title holders (1).
  - **European Performance Spots (2):** the two associations with the highest club coefficient
    in the previous season each get one extra direct spot (2025/26: Newcastle — England, Villarreal — Spain).
  - **Champions League winner rebalancing (1):** if the title holder already qualified via its league,
    the vacant berth goes to the highest-coefficient champion from associations 11–55 (2025/26: Olympiacos).
  - Direct domestic slots by country coefficient (see §1.4), plus 5 from Champions Path and 2 from
    League Path qualifying.
- **2025/26 direct league-phase line-up (example of access list):**
  England 4, Italy 4, Spain 4, Germany 4, France 3, Netherlands 2, Portugal 1, Belgium 1,
  Türkiye 1, Czechia 1, + title holders/performance spots/rebalancing + 7 qualifying winners.

### 1.2 UEFA Europa League (format since 2024/25; current 2025/26)

- **Teams in league phase:** 36, single table.
- **League phase format:** each club plays **8 matches** (4 home, 4 away) against 8 opponents.
- **Qualification:** 1st–8th → R16; 9th–24th → play-offs; 25th–36th eliminated.
- **Knockout:** play-offs → R16 → QF → SF → single-match final (Istanbul 2026).
- **Entry mix (2025/26):** 13 direct, 12 via qualifying, **11 from Champions League**
  (losers of CL Q3 and play-offs). Winner qualifies for the following CL league phase.

### 1.3 UEFA Conference League (format since 2024/25; current 2025/26)

- **Teams in league phase:** 36, single table.
- **League phase format:** each club plays **6 matches** (3 home, 3 away) against 6 opponents,
  one from each of **6 pots of 6**.
- **Qualification:** 1st–8th → R16; 9th–24th → play-offs; 25th–36th eliminated.
- **Knockout:** play-offs → R16 → QF → SF → single-match final.
- **Entry mix (2025/26):** 24 play-off round winners (5 Champions Path + 19 League Path)
  + **12 losers of the Europa League play-off round**. No transfer from EL after the league phase.
  Winner qualifies for the following EL league phase.

### 1.4 UEFA country coefficient & spot allocation (Champions League)

- **Country (association) coefficient:** total points earned by all of an association's clubs in a
  season, divided by the number of clubs that association fielded in the three competitions.
  Uses the rolling **five-season** total.
- **Points per match (league phase onward):** 2 win, 1 draw (halved in qualifying: 1 / 0.5).
- **League-phase bonus points (final league ranking):** CL 1st = 12.000 down to 24th = 6.250,
  25th–36th = 6.000; EL 1st = 6.000 down to 24th = 0.250; ECL 1st = 4.000, 8th = 2.250,
  9th = 2.000 down to 24th = 0.125.
- **Knockout-round bonus:** CL 1.5 per round reached (R16/QF/SF/F); EL 1.0; ECL 0.5.
- **Guaranteed minimums:** EL league phase min 3.0 (not counted in country coefficient);
  ECL league phase min 2.5.
- **Club coefficient:** five-season points total (win/draw + bonuses), used for seeding;
  from 2018/19 a club may instead use its own association's coefficient if higher.
- **CL spot allocation by country ranking (current access list):**
  - Ranks 1–4: **4** league-phase spots.
  - Rank 5: **3** + 1 (Q3).
  - Rank 6: **2** + 1 (Q3).
  - Ranks 7–9: **1** + 1 (Q3).
  - Rank 10: **1** + 1 (Q2).
  - Lower ranks: champions/qualifying rounds down to rank 55 (1 Q1).
- **European Performance Spots (since 2024/25):** the top two associations of the *previous
  season's* coefficient each receive one additional CL league-phase berth for the next-best
  domestic team not already qualified.

Sources: UEFA.com "2025/26 Champions League: Teams, format, dates, draws, final";
BBC Sport "Champions League format" (28 Aug 2025); UEFA.com "2025/26 Europa League / Conference
League: Teams, dates, format"; UEFA club coefficient pages; Wikipedia UEFA competition season pages.

## 2. UEFA — National Teams

### 2.1 UEFA European Championship (Euro) — 2024 and 2028 finals

- **Final tournament teams:** 24.
- **Group stage:** 6 groups of 4; top two + **4 best third-placed** teams → Round of 16.
- **Knockout:** R16 → QF → SF → final; straight knockout, no third-place play-off.
- **Qualification (Euro 2028 format, approved May 2025):**
  - 12 qualifying groups of 4 or 5 teams; **12 group winners + 8 best runners-up** (20 teams) qualify directly.
  - Host nations (England, Rep. Ireland, Scotland, Wales for 2028) participate in qualifying but get
    reserved slots (2 best-ranked hosts not otherwise qualified).
  - Remaining spots via **play-offs** (remaining runners-up + best 2026/27 Nations League group
    winners), 2–4 spots depending on host slots used.

### 2.2 UEFA Nations League (2024/25, and 2026/27)

- **Structure (2024/25):** Leagues A, B, C = **16 teams each** (4 groups of 4); League D = **6 teams**
  (2 groups of 3). Teams allocated by previous overall ranking.
- **League A knockout:** group **winners + runners-up** (8 teams) → two-legged **quarter-finals**
  (March); 4 winners → **Nations League Finals** (semi-finals, third-place, final, June).
- **Promotion/relegation:** group winners of B and C promoted; last-placed of A and B relegated;
  third-placed teams play promotion/relegation play-offs against runners-up from the lower league.
- **2026/27 (fifth edition):** same A/B/C = 16-team, D = 6-team structure, but promotion/relegation
  is structured to converge on a **three-league, 18-team-per-league** system for **2028/29**.

### 2.3 FIFA World Cup qualification — UEFA path (2026)

- **Teams:** 54 UEFA members, drawn into **12 groups** of 4 or 5 teams (single round-robin).
- **Direct slots:** 12 group winners.
- **Play-offs:** 12 runners-up + **4 best Nations League group winners** → 16 teams drawn into
  4 paths (semi-final + final, single leg) → 4 more spots.
- **UEFA total for 2026 World Cup:** 16 direct slots (no inter-confederation play-off berth).

Sources: UEFA.com "Qualification system for UEFA EURO 2028 approved" (21 May 2025); UEFA.com
"EURO 2028: All you need to know"; UEFA.com "2026/27 UEFA Nations League: All you need to know";
Wikipedia UEFA Nations League season pages; FIFA.com World Cup 2026 qualifying explainer.

## 3. FIFA — World Cup

### 3.1 FIFA World Cup 2026 (Canada/Mexico/USA) — 48-team format

- **Teams:** 48 (first expansion from 32).
- **Group stage:** **12 groups of 4** (chosen over 16 groups of 3).
- **Advancement:** top 2 of each group (24 teams) + **8 best third-placed** teams → **Round of 32**
  (new round). Straight single-elimination thereafter to the final.
- **Total matches:** 104 (was 64). Champions play 8 games.
- **Qualification slot allocation by confederation (2026):**

| Confederation | Direct slots | Change vs 2022 | Inter-confederation play-off slots |
|---|---|---|---|
| AFC (Asia) | 8 | +3 | 1 |
| CAF (Africa) | 9 | +4 | 1 |
| CONCACAF (N. America) | 6 (incl. 3 hosts) | +3 | 2 |
| CONMEBOL (S. America) | 6 | +2 | 1 |
| OFC (Oceania) | 1 | +1 (first guaranteed slot) | 1 |
| UEFA (Europe) | 16 | +3 | 0 |

- **FIFA Play-off Tournament:** 6 teams (1 AFC, 1 CAF, 2 CONCACAF, 1 CONMEBOL, 1 OFC) compete
  for the final **2** places (→ 46 direct + 2 = 48).

### 3.2 FIFA Club World Cup 2025 (USA) — 32-team format

- **Teams:** 32, held every **4 years** (was annual 7-team tournament).
- **Group stage:** 8 groups of 4, single round-robin; top 2 → Round of 16.
- **Knockout:** R16 → QF → SF → final, single matches; no third-place play-off.
- **Slot allocation:** UEFA **12**, CONMEBOL **6**, CONCACAF **4** (+1 host via play-in),
  AFC **4**, CAF **4**, OFC **1**, host country **1** = 32.
  (Qualification: confederation Champions League winners 2021–2024 + ranking pathway.)

Sources: FIFA.com "How the FIFA World Cup 26 will work with 48 teams"; FIFA.com "World Cup 2026
groups, qualification rules & tie-breakers" (13 Jun 2026); Wikipedia "2026 FIFA World Cup
qualification"; FIFA.com "FIFA Club World Cup 2025: Teams, dates and format".

## 4. CONMEBOL (South America)

### 4.1 Copa Libertadores (2025)

- **Teams:** 47 from 10 associations.
- **Qualifying:** 3 preliminary stages (19 teams) → 4 winners to group stage.
- **Group stage:** **32 teams**, 8 groups of 4, double round-robin (6 matches each).
- **Advancement:** group winners + runners-up (16) → Round of 16; **third-placed teams (8) drop
  into the Copa Sudamericana knockout play-offs**.
- **Knockout:** R16 → QF → SF (two legs) → single-match **final** (neutral venue, since 2019).
- **Per-country berths:** Brazil **7**, Argentina **6**, the other 8 associations **4 each**
  (plus title holders Copa Libertadores & Copa Sudamericana).

### 4.2 Copa Sudamericana (2025)

- **Group stage:** **32 teams**, 8 groups of 4.
- **Entry mix:** 12 direct entrants + 16 first-stage winners + **4 losers of Libertadores 3rd stage**.
- **Advancement:** 8 group winners → Round of 16; 8 runners-up → knockout play-offs
  (vs the 8 Libertadores third-placed teams). Then R16 → QF → SF → single-match final.

### 4.3 Copa América (2024)

- **Teams:** 16 — 10 CONMEBOL + 6 CONCACAF (guests).
- **Group stage:** 4 groups of 4; top 2 → quarter-finals.
- **Knockout:** QF → SF → third-place play-off → final. (Pre-final rounds: no extra time, straight
  to penalties; final: extra time then penalties.)

Sources: Wikipedia "2025 Copa Libertadores" (and group/qualifying stage pages); Wikipedia
"2025 Copa Sudamericana group stage"; ESPN "Copa América: Bracket and results from 2024 finals";
Wikipedia "2024 Copa América".

## 5. AFC (Asia)

### 5.1 AFC Champions League Elite (2025/26; expands 2026/27)

- **Teams (league stage, 2025/26):** **24** (from 12 associations), split into **two leagues of 12**
  — West and East regions.
- **League stage format:** each club plays **8 matches** (4 home, 4 away) against 8 opponents
  within its region.
- **Advancement:** top 8 of each league (16) → Round of 16 (regional), then QF → SF → **final**
  (centralized final stage in Saudi Arabia for the first editions).
- **Slot allocation:** by AFC club competitions ranking; top-ranked Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar get
  multiple league-stage slots; lower associations enter qualifying play-offs.
- **From 2026/27:** league stage expands to **32** teams (16 East + 16 West).
- Winner qualifies for FIFA Intercontinental Cup and the next FIFA Club World Cup.

### 5.2 AFC Champions League Two (2025/26) — second tier

- **Teams (group stage):** **32** (16 West + 16 East) from up to 24 associations.
- **Group stage:** 8 groups of 4 (4 per region), double round-robin; **top 2 of each group → Round of 16**.
- Predecessor: AFC Cup (rebranded 2024/25). Winner gets a preliminary-stage path to the following ACL Elite.
- Third tier: AFC Challenge League.

### 5.3 AFC Asian Cup (2027, Saudi Arabia)

- **Teams:** 24 (expanded from 16 in 2019).
- **Group stage:** 6 groups of 4; top 2 + **4 best third-placed** → Round of 16.
- **Knockout:** straight knockout to final; no third-place play-off.
- **Qualification:** 18 teams from the second round qualify directly; final 6 via a third round
  (6 groups of 4, group winners qualify).

Sources: AFC "AFC Champions League Elite 2025/26 Competition Regulations"; Wikipedia
"2025–26 AFC Champions League Elite" and "AFC Champions League Elite"; Wikipedia "2025–26 AFC
Champions League Two"; AFC/the-AFC.com ACL Two group stage draw; Wikipedia "2027 AFC Asian Cup".

## 6. CAF (Africa)

### 6.1 CAF Champions League (2025/26)

- **Qualifying:** 2 preliminary (knockout) rounds; a record 62 clubs entered preliminary rounds.
- **Group stage:** **16 teams**, 4 groups of 4, double round-robin (6 matches each).
- **Advancement:** top 2 of each group → quarter-finals.
- **Knockout:** QF → SF (two legs) → final.
- Seeding by **CAF 5-year ranking**; winner qualifies for FIFA Intercontinental Cup and FIFA Club World Cup.

### 6.2 CAF Confederation Cup (2025/26) — second tier

- **Group stage:** **16 teams**, 4 groups of 4; top 2 → quarter-finals; two-legged knockout to final.
- (Same group-stage skeleton as the Champions League.)

### 6.3 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) — 2025 (Morocco) and 2027

- **Teams (2025):** 24 (expanded from 16 in 2019).
- **Group stage:** 6 groups of 4; top 2 + **4 best third-placed** → Round of 16; straight knockout.
- **2027 (Kenya/Tanzania/Uganda):** expands to **28 teams** → 7 groups of 4; top 2 per group (14)
  + **2 best third-placed** → Round of 16.

Sources: cafonline.com "Everything you need to know about the 2025/26 TotalEnergies CAF Champions
League"; Wikipedia "2025–26 CAF Champions League group stage"; cafonline.com AFCON 2025 media guide
& final-draw release; Morocco World News "2027 AFCON to Expand to 28 Teams" (23 Jul 2026).

## 7. CONCACAF (North/Central America & Caribbean)

### 7.1 CONCACAF Champions Cup (2025)

- **Teams:** 27, played as a **direct-elimination knockout** (no group stage).
- **Rounds:** Round One (22 clubs) → Round of 16 (5 bye clubs + 11 winners) → QF → SF → **Final**.
- **Legs:** first four rounds home-and-away (aggregate); final is single match.
- **Qualification paths:** Leagues Cup (N. America), Central American Cup, Caribbean Cup, plus
  domestic leagues/cups of Canada, Mexico, USA.

### 7.2 CONCACAF Nations League (2024/25)

- **Three leagues:** A (16 teams), B (16 teams), C (9 teams).
- **League A:** top 4 ranked teams (Mexico, USA, Panama, Canada) receive a bye; the other 12 play in
  2 groups of 6 in a **"Swiss-style"** system (4 matches each, 2 home/2 away). Group winners +
  runners-up join the 4 byes in **home-and-away quarter-finals**; 4 winners → **Finals**
  (semi-finals, third-place, final).
- **League B:** 4 groups of 4, double round-robin.
- **League C:** 3 groups of 3, double round-robin.
- **Promotion/relegation:** League A 5th/6th relegated; League B group winners promoted, 4th relegated;
  League C group winners + best runner-up promoted.

### 7.3 Gold Cup (2025)

- **Teams:** 16 (15 CONCACAF + 1 guest — Saudi Arabia 2025).
- **Group stage:** 4 groups of 4; top 2 → quarter-finals; straight knockout to final.
- **Qualification:** 8 teams via CNL placement; remaining via Gold Cup Prelims.

Sources: Concacaf.com "Twenty-seven clubs confirmed for 2025 Concacaf Champions Cup";
Concacaf.com "2024/25 Concacaf Nations League: All You Need to Know"; Wikipedia "2024–25 CONCACAF
Nations League"; Concacaf.com "2025 Gold Cup" host-cities/format release.

## 8. OFC (Oceania)

### 8.1 OFC Men's Champions League (2025)

- **Teams entering:** up to 18 from 11 associations; smaller nations go through a **qualifying stage**
  (preliminary group, 2025 in Cook Islands) with 1 winner advancing.
- **Group stage:** **8 teams**, 2 groups of 4, single round-robin at a centralised venue.
- **Advancement:** group winners + runners-up (4) → **semi-finals → final** (single matches).
- Winner qualifies for FIFA Intercontinental Cup / FIFA Club World Cup.

### 8.2 OFC Men's Nations Cup (2024)

- **Teams:** 8 — 7 direct + 1 qualifying winner.
- **Qualifying:** the 4 lowest-ranked nations play a round-robin for the last spot.
- **Finals:** 2 groups of 4; top 2 → semi-finals → final (plus 3rd/5th/7th-place play-offs).

Sources: OFC "Regulations OFC Men's Nations Cup 2024"; Wikipedia "2025 OFC Men's Champions League"
(group stage & knockout stage).

## 9. Ranking / Coefficient Mechanics

### 9.1 FIFA/Coca-Cola World Ranking — "SUM" algorithm (since August 2018)

- **Formula:** `P = Pbefore + I × (W − We)`
  - `Pbefore` — points before the match.
  - `I` — match importance (see table).
  - `W` — result: 1 = win, 0.5 = draw, 0 = loss.
  - `We` — expected result: `We = 1 / (10^(−dr/600) + 1)`, where `dr` = rating difference
    (Pbefore of Team A − Pbefore of Team B).
- **Importance weights (`I`):**

| I | Match type |
|---|---|
| 5 | Friendly outside International Match Calendar window |
| 10 | Friendly inside window |
| 15 | Nations League group phase |
| 25 | Nations League play-offs/finals; WC & confederation final qualifiers |
| 35 | Confederation final tournament up to QF |
| 40 | Confederation final tournament QF onward |
| 50 | FIFA World Cup up to QF |
| 60 | FIFA World Cup QF onward |

- Points are added/subtracted (Elo-style) rather than averaged; **knockout-stage losses in final
  competitions are excluded** from the calculation.

### 9.2 UEFA coefficients (club and country)

- **Country coefficient:** (sum of all clubs' points in a season) ÷ (number of clubs fielded),
  summed over 5 seasons. Determines how many places each association gets and at which round.
- **Club coefficient:** 5-season points total (used for seeding); a club may use its association's
  coefficient if higher (from 2018/19).
- **Match points (league phase onward):** 2 win / 1 draw / 0 loss (halved in qualifying).
- **League-phase bonus (by final position):** CL 12.0 (1st) → 6.25 (24th), 6.0 (25th–36th);
  EL 6.0 (1st) → 0.25 (24th); ECL 4.0 (1st) → 2.0 (9th) → 0.125 (24th).
- **Knockout-round bonus:** CL 1.5 / EL 1.0 / ECL 0.5 per round reached (R16, QF, SF, F).
- **European Performance Spots (2024/25 onward):** two associations with the best *single-season*
  coefficient each receive one extra CL league-phase berth the following season.

Sources: FIFA "Revision of the FIFA/Coca-Cola World Ranking" (PDF); FIFA.com World Ranking procedures;
UEFA.com "How men's club coefficients are calculated" (Annex D); UEFA Champions League regulations
Annex B; BBC Sport "Uefa coefficient: How does it affect Champions League places?" (19 Aug 2025).
